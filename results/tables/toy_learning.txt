Toy Attention Learning Experiment
=================================

Task:
Predict the average of the final two values in a synthetic sequence.

Sequence length:
6

Training samples:
240

Validation samples:
60

Attention:
First-principles scaled dot-product attention.

Training:
100 epochs

Learning rate:
0.05

Trainable component:
Output prediction head.

Attention projections:
Fixed during this experiment.

Results:

Epoch 10:
Train MSE = 0.624928
Validation MSE = 0.542871
Validation MAE = 0.596022

Epoch 50:
Train MSE = 0.576582
Validation MSE = 0.521620
Validation MAE = 0.585334

Epoch 100:
Train MSE = 0.527566
Validation MSE = 0.496329
Validation MAE = 0.569101

Final Validation MAE:
0.569101

Final Validation RMSE:
0.704507

Observation:
The validation loss and MAE decreased consistently during training,
showing that the attention representation can participate in a
learning pipeline. Convergence was gradual rather than unstable.

Limitation:
The attention projection matrices were fixed and only the output
prediction head was optimized. Therefore, this experiment demonstrates
learning on top of attention rather than end-to-end optimization of
all attention parameters.
