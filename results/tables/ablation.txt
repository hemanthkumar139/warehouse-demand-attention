Scaled vs Unscaled Attention Ablation
=====================================

Hypothesis:
Scaling QK^T by sqrt(d_k) should reduce the magnitude of attention
logits and reduce softmax saturation, especially for larger d_k.

Results:

d_k=4
Scaled entropy: 0.1636
Unscaled entropy: 0.1356
Unscaled max attention: approximately 1.0

d_k=16
Scaled entropy: 0.1449
Unscaled entropy: 0.0170
Unscaled max attention: 1.0

d_k=64
Scaled entropy: 0.1491
Unscaled entropy: 0.0012
Unscaled max attention: 1.0

d_k=128
Scaled entropy: 0.0427
Unscaled entropy: 0.0587
Unscaled max attention: 1.0

Observation:
For d_k=16 and d_k=64, unscaled attention produced substantially
larger logits and much lower attention entropy than scaled attention.
This indicates stronger softmax concentration and potential saturation.

Interpretation:
The dot product QK^T tends to grow in magnitude as the key/query
dimension increases. Dividing by sqrt(d_k) controls this growth before
softmax, keeping the logits in a more manageable range.

Limitation:
The random initialization creates variability, and the d_k=128 result
does not show a simple monotonic entropy relationship. Therefore the
evidence supports reduced logit growth and often reduced saturation,
rather than a claim that scaling always produces higher entropy.
